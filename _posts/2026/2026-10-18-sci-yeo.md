Between teaching and welcoming new starters managed to also get a day at [Yeo Valley](https://www.yeovalley.co.uk/the-canteen) installing our latest UNO Q Acoupi setup and a visit to the Science Museum with our new CE students. Is always fun to walk around the Information Age exhibit to be reminded of where we have come from and enjoyed spending a the day over on Exhibition doing meetings out of the V&A cafe's and sculpture rooms.
