Great to be back out and about in London for IoT + Physical AI events this week: first off we hosted the [London IoT meet-up in the CASA lab](https://lnkd.in/p/ed63KaS6), and I managed to get cross to the [Inanimate product launch](https://inanimate.tech) in Shoreditch to see what Matt and Daniel have been developing. The IoT Meet-Up was the 15-year anniversary and the last edition with [Alex](https://lnkd.in/p/eM_uRUpE) stepping down as organiser after a decade and a half of bringing the community together. Was lovely to catch up with some old faces and new, Adrian McEwen, Nick O'Leary, Joanna Montgomery, Dimitrios Spiliopoulos, Boris Adryan and Mark Setrem, and we had products from nearly every year on display. Felt good to be able to host it in the CASA lab and show our students what the wider community looks like.

<!--more-->

The Inanimate studio was packed with a constant stream of people arriving. Some really nice demos on display, and I loved the product posters as a way of describing what each device does. Enjoyed a serendipitous chat with CASA alumni Vish and got a sneak peek at a prototype gen 3 camera. Looking forward to using these projects as case studies with our students on how products actually get developed.

Spent some time testing a Knowles SPC0641LU4H MEMS mic on a breakout board made by Martin (based on this [Robot Shop breakout board](https://uk.robotshop.com/products/digital-mems-microphone-breakout-board-sph0641lu4h-1)) with the UnoQ — [datasheet](https://www.mouser.com/catalog/specsheets/know-s-a0010889928-1.pdf). Early days, more soon, but am trying to get under 10Wh per day if I can.
