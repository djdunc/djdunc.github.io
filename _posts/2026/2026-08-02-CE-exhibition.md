Managed to get around to photographing all the CASA CE 2026 exhibition projects this week. Some lovely projects again this year and great to see the students work come together over a frenetic two weeks leading up to the install week. 

<!--more-->

Leah and Simon did a great job again of curating the space and was lovely to show a few visitors around this past week. Each time I was in the space there was a few people looking around the work.
