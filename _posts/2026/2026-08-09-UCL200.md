Almost there with the installation of sensors on the UCL200 Greenwall. Lovely hot weather to be install sensors in the full sun. Weather station up, 3 devices running bioacoustics (acoupi bird, acoupi batdetect2 and birdnet-pi) plus a camera looking at the greenwall. Soil sensors installed and reporting to The Things Network and people counters installed under the canopy to measure dwell times. 

<!--more-->

Sensors are alive and sending data to our MQTT broker. A website with access to the data feeds will follow shortly.
