They say:
> "Station South CIC was formed in 2017 by local residents to prevent the old Levenshulme station building falling into disrepair. With support from the local community, Station South is now a destination cycle café, bar, workshop, urban garden and community hangout based in a beautifully restored train station.

> Station South is focused on the community, with cycling and community programmes running throughout the year. Our bar, cafe and bicycle repair workshop allow us to operate sustainably as a business, reinvesting into our local initiatives, run directly from Station South.

> In 2026 we have even bigger plans, made possible by. every single person who comes in for brunch, buys a drink, books a bike service, volunteers at one of our events, or joins one of our programmes. By supporting us you're supporting our community programme to ensure Station South is for Everyone."
