A trip up north to see family meant some lovely walks along the coast and through the turning trees. Also meant some lovely fish and chips with mushy peas at [Royal Fisheries](http://www.royalfisherieswhitby.co.uk/home/)! Cohort 6 CE students finished their first two modules this week. Prototyping the IoT students all presented their Vespera controllers, some pretty cool prototypes given the minimal amount of time to pick the skills to create an end to end IoT system.
