A week of dissertation project support and working on solar powered bioacoustic sensors. Visited House Mill to re-install Satria's sonar sensor (dissertation project from 2024) - the facade looks really impressive after its paint job. The picture above shows the lora sensor and in the distance the top of the Orbit in QEOP that the sensor is connecting to (about 1.3km). Enjoyed the visit to Houses of Parliament for the Can AI save the planet event on the terrace.
